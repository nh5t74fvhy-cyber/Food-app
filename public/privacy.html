Your data on MealRadar

Updated September 21, 2026.

You can browse offers without an account. Saved offers and preferences use this browser’s local storage. Clearing site data removes those local copies.

Accounts

When you create a MealRadar account, Supabase processes your email and credentials for authentication. Signed-in favorites and preferences are stored in our Supabase database, with access rules that separate users’ records. Vercel hosts the website and may process technical request information to operate the service.

Location and notifications

Nearby search and scheduled notifications are not active yet. When activated, nearby search requires permission and sends coordinates to our server and Google Places to find restaurants. It does not track you in the background. Enabling push would store your device’s push subscription and chosen meal times. You can deny browser permissions.

Advertising and restaurant links

No display advertising network is currently active. Restaurant offer links open the restaurant’s website, where that site’s policies apply. If we add paid placements or affiliate links, they will be labeled. A sponsored click may be measured by the destination partner. We do not sell a user wallet or store banking or payout details in this app.

Managing your information

You can remove saved deals and update preferences in the app. For questions about an account or removal of account data, contact the project owner through the project. Do not post passwords, private account details or banking information in a public issue.
